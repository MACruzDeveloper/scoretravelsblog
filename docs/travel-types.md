# Travel types

Ideas for experience categories. The categories in use are managed from `/admin/categories`.

## By purpose

- Leisure and tourism: rest, exploration, holidays
- Work or business: meetings, conferences, training
- Studies: courses abroad, exchanges, Erasmus
- Health: medical tourism, spas, treatments
- Religious or spiritual: pilgrimages, retreats
- Adventure and sport: expeditions, competitions, extreme routes
- Gastronomic: trips focused on cuisine and food culture
- Cultural: museums, festivals, historical heritage
- Volunteering: international cooperation, humanitarian work
- Visiting family or friends

## By duration

- Weekend getaway
- Short trip (less than a week)
- Long trip or grand journey (weeks or months)
- Long-stay trip or expatriation

## By type of organisation

- Organised trip (travel package)
- Independent or backpacker trip
- Group trip
- Solo trip

## By company

- Alone, as a couple, with family, with friends, with pets

## By main transport

- Road trip (car), cruise, interrail (train), motorcycle trip, on foot (long-distance hiking)
